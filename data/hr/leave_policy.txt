Leave Policy

Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly and unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.

Sick Leave
Employees may take up to 10 days of sick leave per year with a valid medical certificate.
For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note is mandatory.

Maternity & Paternity Leave
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave.
- Paternity leave: 2 weeks of paid leave.

How to Apply
1. Log in to the HR Portal (https://hr.company.com)
2. Submit leave request at least 7 days in advance for planned leave.
3. Manager approval is required.

For emergency leave, contact HR at hr@company.com.
