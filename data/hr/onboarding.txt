New Employee Onboarding Guide

First Day
- Report to the reception desk by 9:00 AM.
- Collect your employee ID badge and laptop from IT.
- Complete the mandatory security training module.

First Week Checklist
- Set up company email and Slack account
- Complete HR paperwork (tax forms, bank details)
- Meet your buddy / mentor
- Review the Employee Handbook
- Attend the company orientation session (usually Wednesday)

Important Contacts
- HR Team: hr@company.com
- IT Helpdesk: it-support@company.com
- Your Manager: Check your welcome email

Welcome to the team!
