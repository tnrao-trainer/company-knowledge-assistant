Employee Benefits Overview

Health Insurance
Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage for employees and dependents.
Coverage starts on the first day of employment.

Retirement Plan
Company matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to 4% of salary.

Learning & Development
Annual learning budget of $1,500 per employee for courses, conferences, and certifications.

Parental Leave
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid leave
- Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks paid leave

Other Perks
- Free lunch on Fridays
- Monthly team outings
- Home office stipend of $300 for new remote employees
- Unlimited coffee and snacks in the office
