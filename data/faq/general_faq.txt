Company FAQ

Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Office Locations
- Headquarters: 123 Innovation Drive, Tech City
- Branch offices in New York, London, and Singapore

How do I request a laptop?
Submit a ticket through the IT Service Desk portal (https://servicedesk.company.com).

What is the dress code?
Business casual. Jeans and smart casual attire are acceptable. Formal wear is required only for client meetings.

Who do I contact for payroll issues?
Email payroll@company.com or raise a ticket in the HR portal.

Is there a gym or wellness benefit?
Yes. Employees receive a monthly wellness stipend of $50 that can be used for gym memberships or fitness apps.
