Password Reset Guide

Self-Service Password Reset
1. Go to https://reset.company.com
2. Enter your Employee ID or company email address
3. You will receive a One-Time Password (OTP) on your registered email
4. Enter the OTP and create a new password

Password Requirements
- Minimum 12 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)

If You Are Locked Out
Contact the IT Helpdesk:
- Email: it-support@company.com
- Slack channel: #it-helpdesk
- Phone: Extension 5555

Do not share your password with anyone.
