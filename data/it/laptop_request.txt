How to Request a Laptop or Hardware

Process
1. Go to the IT Service Desk portal: https://servicedesk.company.com
2. Click "New Request" → "Hardware"
3. Select "Laptop Request" or the specific device needed
4. Fill in the business justification
5. Submit for manager approval

Standard Laptops
- MacBook Pro 14" (Engineering / Design)
- Dell XPS 15 (General staff)
- ThinkPad T14 (Finance / Admin)

Expected Timeline
- Standard request: 5–7 business days
- Urgent request (with VP approval): 2 business days

Returning Equipment
When leaving the company, return all hardware to IT on your last working day.
